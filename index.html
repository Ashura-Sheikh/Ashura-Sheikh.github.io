Who is Sheikh

I'm a Senior Programme & Operations Leader with 17+ years leading global programmes across Operations, Quality, and Technical Program Management — currently running Development Quality & Reliability for a global vendor operation spanning a 15-member cross-functional team and 4 vendor partnerships.

My real edge is combining operational leadership with hands-on technical skill: I build automation in Python, JavaScript, SQL, and REST APIs to solve problems process fixes alone can't reach, and I've been designing and running multi-agent AI systems — not just using AI tools, but building the governance, review, and self-correction mechanisms that make them reliable enough to run real operational work.

I like problems that are genuinely ambiguous: scaling a team without breaking quality, automating work nobody else wanted to touch, or figuring out how AI agents should actually be supervised rather than just deployed. If it needs both judgment and a working solution, that's where I do my best work.
